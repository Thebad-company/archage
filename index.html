02 · The Architecture

A Choreography of Glass and Stone

Inspired by the rugged purity of the Himalayas, our villas are a dialogue between architectural precision and raw nature.

Using locally sourced quartz stone and expansive low-E glass, we have created structures that don't just sit on the land—they belong to it. The design prioritizes the view as the primary 'artwork' of every room.

Cantilevered Decks: Engineered to hover over the lake-side slopes.

Double-Height Living: A volume of space that matches the scale of the valley.

Thermal Massing: Intelligent stone placement for natural climate regulation.

07 · The Tehri Canvas

A Sea in the Sky

Tehri Lake is not just a body of water; it is a micro-climate of calm. With 52 square kilometers of surface area, it offers a scale of waterfront living previously unknown in the Indian hills.

At 1,800 meters, the air is thin, crisp, and perpetually cool. The massive thermal mass of the lake regulates the surrounding valley, creating a perpetual spring. It is the only place in the Himalayas where you can watch the sunset over a horizon of blue water.
