07 · The Tehri Canvas

A Sea in the Sky

Tehri Lake is not just a body of water; it is a micro-climate of calm. With 52 square kilometers of surface area, it offers a scale of waterfront living previously unknown in the Indian hills.

At 1,800 meters, the air is thin, crisp, and perpetually cool. The massive thermal mass of the lake regulates the surrounding valley, creating a perpetual spring. It is the only place in the Himalayas where you can watch the sunset over a horizon of blue water.
